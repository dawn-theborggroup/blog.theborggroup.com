What is the Southwest Florida housing market like today?

Updated November 9, 2026

Every morning we pull the latest Southwest Florida MLS numbers for the areas we serve and put them here, in plain English: how many homes and condos are for sale, how many are under contract, what they're selling for and how close to asking price. Single-family homes and condos are shown separately, because they're two very different markets.

Quick Answer

As of November 9, 2026, the Southwest Florida areas we serve have about 11,088 single-family homes and condos for sale across Naples, Bonita Springs, Bonita Beach, Estero, Fort Myers, Fort Myers Beach and Cape Coral. The median single-family sold price over the last 45 days (30 in Cape Coral) runs from $395,000 in Cape Coral to $800,000 in Naples. For condos, it runs from $170,000 in Cape Coral to $455,000 in Naples. Homes are selling for a median of 94.5% to 97.9% of their final list price, which means most buyers are negotiating, and pricing right still matters for sellers.

What do these numbers mean for buyers and sellers?

Area-wide numbers set the stage, but they don't price a home. Two neighborhoods a mile apart can move at very different speeds, and the gap between list price and sold price changes from one community and price range to the next. If you're thinking about selling, the number that matters is what homes like yours, in your community, have actually closed for. If you're buying, it's knowing where there's room to negotiate and where good homes still go fast. Since 2005, Mark and Dawn have helped Southwest Florida buyers and sellers read the market street by street.

Where do these numbers come from?

They come from Southwest Florida MLS listings for each area, refreshed every morning. Single-family counts include detached homes and leave out duplexes and manufactured homes; condo counts include condos and attached villas and townhomes. A listing that matches more than one search is counted once. This page is information and knowledge only. The numbers are deemed reliable but not guaranteed and can change throughout the day.
