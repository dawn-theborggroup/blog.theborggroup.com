How much will Amendment 3 save you, whether you stay or move?

Florida voters approved Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026, raising the homestead exemption on non-school property taxes to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. Your real savings also depend on Save Our Homes, the cap that keeps your assessed value below market value, and on portability if you move. Quick Answer

If you have a Florida homestead, Amendment 3 raises your exemption on non-school property taxes from about $51,411 to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. In Lee County, at the average non-school rate of about 10.65 mills, that saves roughly $1,050 a year in 2027 and $2,115 a year from 2028 on a home assessed at $250,000 or more. Save Our Homes and portability don't change: your assessed value can still rise no more than 3% (or inflation) a year, and if you move within Florida you can carry up to $500,000 of that benefit to your next homestead.

How do Save Our Homes and Amendment 3 work together?

They stack. Save Our Homes lowers the value you're taxed on by capping how fast your assessed value can rise, so long-time owners are often assessed well below market value. Amendment 3 then takes a bigger exemption off that assessed value for county, city and district taxes. School taxes still use the $25,000 exemption, which doesn't change. The longer you've owned your home, the more Save Our Homes is doing for you, and Amendment 3 adds to it.

What happens to my taxes if I sell and buy another Florida home?

Your new home's assessed value starts at its market value, but portability lets you bring your Save Our Homes benefit with you, up to $500,000. Moving up, you transfer the full benefit. Downsizing, you transfer the same percentage of value it protected before. Because you were already a Florida resident before December 31, 2026, you keep the larger Amendment 3 exemption on the new home too. You have to apply for both the homestead exemption and portability by March 1 of the first year, and you have up to three tax years after leaving your old homestead to use portability. Learn more in our guide to Save Our Homes portability.

Does Amendment 3 help second homes and rentals?

Not with the larger exemption, which is only for a primary residence. Non-homestead property gets a different benefit: starting in 2027, the yearly cap on increases in its assessed value for non-school taxes drops from 10% to 5%. School taxes on non-homestead property stay uncapped.

Where do I find my assessed and market values?

On the TRIM notice mailed every August, or on the Lee County or Collier County Property Appraiser's website by searching your address. Look for "market" or "just" value and "assessed" value. Our guide to reading your TRIM notice shows where each number is.

Should Amendment 3 change when I sell or buy?

It can change the math, and timing matters. Whether you're a resident by December 31, 2026, when your new homestead starts, how much Save Our Homes benefit you'd carry over, and what your next home costs to insure all affect what moving really costs you each year.
