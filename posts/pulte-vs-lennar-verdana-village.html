Pulte vs. Lennar at Verdana Village: which builder is right for you?

By Mark & Dawn Borg·October 5, 2026·8 min read

Pulte or Lennar at Verdana Village? Choose Lennar if you want clear pricing, fewer decisions, and upgrades already included. Choose Pulte if you want more say in the design through its design studio, or you need a Quick Move-In home on a tight timeline. Neither builder is better across the board. They sell the same community in two different ways, and the right one depends on how you like to buy.

Verdana Village is unusual because you're choosing a builder philosophy as much as a floor plan. Lennar and Pulte are both building in the community at the same time, with overlapping prices and very different buying experiences. We've walked buyers through both many times, and this is how we frame it.

How does Lennar build at Verdana Village?

Lennar uses what it calls "Everything's Included." The upgrades in the model home are standard in every home rather than priced as add-ons. That keeps pricing transparent and cuts down on the upgrade-room experience some buyers find stressful. Lennar offers three series at Verdana Village:

  • Villa Series: attached two-story villas, including the Magnolia (3 bed, 2 bath, 1,417 sq. ft.) and the Orchid (2 bed, 2 bath, 1,564 sq. ft.).
  • Executive Series: single-family homes, from the Venice (3 bed, 3 bath, 1,849 sq. ft.) up to the Monte Carlo (5 bed, 3 bath, 3,231 sq. ft.).
  • Manor Series: larger single-family homes, topping out at the Sorrento (5 bed, 4 bath, 3,283 sq. ft.).

How does Pulte build at Verdana Village?

Pulte is more design-driven. You work through a design studio to choose finishes, layouts, and options. That means more decisions, but also more personalization. Pulte also offers Quick Move-In (QMI) homes, which are already under construction or complete, a good option if you can't wait for a full build. Its series at Verdana Village:

  • Scenic Series: starting at 1,405+ sq. ft. in 2 bed, 2 bath layouts.
  • Executive Series: starting at 1,670+ sq. ft.
  • Echelon Series: starting at 2,269+ sq. ft. in 3 bed, 3 bath layouts.

Pricing and incentives change often by builder, floor plan, and lot, so call us for current numbers on both.

What stays the same no matter which builder you pick?

You're buying into the same community, so the amenities and the community costs are shared. That includes the Indoor Sports Complex, the resort pool, the restaurant, and the shopping just outside the gates. The HOA is set by home series, about $342 a month for Villa, $387 for Executive, and $393 for Manor, and the annual CDD runs roughly $1,872 to $2,218 and appears on your Lee County tax bill. There's also a $480 annual food and beverage minimum. When you compare a Pulte home to a Lennar home, ask which fee tier it falls under, because the same square footage can carry different costs. For the full fee picture, see our Verdana Village guide and our CDD fees guide.

So which one should you pick?

  • Pick Lennar if you'd rather know what you're paying up front, don't want to spend weekends in a design center, and like the idea of included upgrades.
  • Pick Pulte if you have strong opinions about finishes and layouts, or you need a home that's ready sooner through Quick Move-In.
  • Compare both if you're between series. The price ranges overlap, so a Pulte Executive and a Lennar Executive can land close together with different feature sets.

One rule applies to both: register your own agent on your first visit. Builder contracts are written to protect the builder, and the sales team represents the builder, not you. An independent buyer's agent reviews the contract, helps you time incentives, and protects your deposit structure. The builder typically pays the agent's commission, so it generally costs you nothing. Skip it and you may lose the right to representation on that purchase. Common questions

Is Pulte or Lennar better at Verdana Village?

Neither is better across the board. Lennar's Everything's Included model keeps pricing transparent with fewer decisions, while Pulte offers more design customization and Quick Move-In homes. Choose based on how you like to buy and how soon you need to move in.

What does Everything's Included mean at Lennar?

It means the upgrades shown in the model home are standard in every Lennar home at Verdana Village rather than priced as optional add-ons. That makes pricing more transparent, though you'll want to confirm exactly what's included in the specific floor plan you're considering.

What is a Quick Move-In home at Pulte?

A Quick Move-In home is one that's already under construction or complete, so you can close sooner than on a home built from scratch. Pulte has offered incentives on select Quick Move-In homes at Verdana Village, and those incentives change often, so confirm current offers.

Do Pulte and Lennar homes at Verdana Village cost the same?

Their price ranges overlap, but entry points and floor plans differ, and incentives change often. Call us for current pricing on both builders, and compare specific floor plans and lots rather than series names.

Should I bring my own agent to the Verdana Village sales center?

Yes. Register your agent on your first visit, before you talk to the sales team. The builder's representative works for the builder, and the builder typically pays your agent's commission, so having your own representation generally costs you nothing.
