The Verandah Fort Myers FL: buyer's guide to homes, costs & golf

By Mark & Dawn Borg·September 21, 2026·8 min read

What is The Verandah community in Fort Myers, FL?

The Verandah is a 1,456-acre gated, master-planned golf and riverside community in East Fort Myers (zip 33905), situated along more than a mile and a half of the Orange River. It features two 18-hole championship golf courses — Old Orange (designed by Bob Cupp) and Whispering Oak (designed by Jack Nicklaus and Jack Nicklaus II) — with optional, non-mandatory golf membership. Homes range from the mid-$300s for new construction to over $1.2 million for resale single-family, with HOA fees typically running $639–$900 per month depending on neighborhood and home type.

If you're comparing golf communities in Fort Myers and The Verandah keeps coming up in your research, there's a reason. It's one of the largest and most amenity-rich master-planned communities in Lee County — and unlike many golf neighborhoods, it doesn't require you to join the club to live there.

Here's what buyers need to know before they schedule a showing.

Location, Size, and What You're Getting Into

The Verandah sits on State Road 80 about two miles east of I-75 in the 33905 zip code — what locals sometimes call East Fort Myers or Buckingham. It spans 1,456 acres, with roughly two-thirds of that set aside as open space: preserve land, wetlands, lakes, and more than nine miles of trails and boardwalks that wind through the property.

This isn't a compact, densely built community. The Verandah was designed to feel like you're living inside a nature preserve — mature oak trees, Spanish moss, and the Orange River running along the perimeter. If your idea of a great morning involves a kayak launch before 8 a.m. and coffee at the riverfront restaurant by 9, you're going to understand the appeal immediately.

The community is planned for approximately 1,700 homes at full build-out, spread across more than 20 distinct neighborhoods. You've got coach homes on the lower end of the size spectrum, attached villas in the middle, and single-family homes that range from modest three-bedrooms to sprawling riverfront estates. Neal Communities is the primary active builder right now, with new construction starting in the upper $200s and quick move-in homes currently listed from roughly $380,000 to $637,000 — some with significant builder incentives.

Resale inventory typically runs from the mid-$400s to $1.2 million-plus, with a median price around $495,000 as of mid-2026. Average price per square foot runs approximately $257 based on recent sales data.

The Golf Situation: Two Courses, One Important Detail

Two championship courses sit inside the gates:

  • Old Orange — an 18-hole course designed by Bob Cupp, known for its classic layout through the community's natural landscape
  • Whispering Oak — co-designed by Jack Nicklaus and Jack Nicklaus II, which Golf Inc. Magazine has ranked among the top private courses in Florida

Both are operated through the Verandah Golf Club, which also runs a full practice facility, putting greens, a short-game area, and junior instruction programs.

Here's the detail that matters most for buyers who are on the fence about golf: membership is optional. You don't have to join to live here. This puts The Verandah in a different category from communities like Fiddlesticks Country Club, where golf membership is mandatory, or from bundled communities where the cost is baked into your purchase price whether you play or not.

If you're a golfer, this is great news — you get access to two top-tier private courses without being locked into a membership structure you didn't choose. If you're not a golfer, you can live in a beautiful, amenity-rich community without paying for 36 holes you'll never use.

Golf membership costs are not publicly listed and will require a direct conversation with the Verandah Golf Club's membership office. For an accurate current number, contact Ellen Singer at (239) 710-3678 — she's the Director of Membership and can walk you through available tiers and current initiation structures.

Amenities Beyond the Golf Course

The Verandah has enough going on that non-golfers don't feel like they're living in someone else's community. The amenity package includes:

  • Five lighted Har-Tru tennis courts and pickleball courts
  • Resort-style pool and full-service spa
  • State-of-the-art fitness center with group studio classes
  • The River House — a waterfront restaurant and lounge with views of the Orange River
  • A riverfront boathouse with a general store and canoe and kayak launch
  • Dog park, bocce courts, and an art studio
  • Full-time activities director coordinating social clubs, events, and community gatherings
  • Nine miles of walking and biking trails through natural preserves

The Orange River access is genuinely special. Residents regularly kayak alongside manatees (especially during cooler months when the warm river water draws them in), spot river otters and herons along the boardwalk, and fish from the community's riverfront spots. If you have a private boat, you can access the Intracoastal Waterway from the community's marina.

Manatee Park is just minutes away on Palm Beach Boulevard — one of the best spots in Southwest Florida to see manatees in the wild.

What You'll Pay: HOA Fees, Taxes, and Cost Context

Budget conversations about The Verandah need to account for a few layers of cost.

HOA fees run approximately $639–$900 per month depending on the specific neighborhood and home type. Coach homes and villas that are maintained externally typically sit toward the higher end of the range because landscaping and exterior care are included. Single-family homes may fall closer to the lower end, where you retain more control over your lot but handle more maintenance yourself.

Florida law requires sellers to provide full HOA disclosure documents before closing — that includes the current budget, meeting minutes, reserve fund status, and any pending special assessments. Don't skip this review. The reserve fund health is one of the most important things to evaluate in any HOA community, and it's easy to miss if you're not specifically looking for it. Our recent post on how to evaluate a Florida HOA before you buy walks you through exactly what to look for.

Property taxes in Lee County are assessed on the purchase price (not the previous owner's Save Our Homes-capped value), so your tax bill resets when you buy. The average annual property tax at The Verandah runs approximately $7,800–$8,000 based on recent sales data — though your actual figure will depend on your purchase price and any applicable exemptions.

If this will be your primary residence, Florida's homestead exemption reduces your assessed value by up to $50,000 and caps future annual assessment increases at 3% (or CPI, whichever is lower) through the Save Our Homes provision. Apply by March 1 of the year following your purchase through the Lee County Property Appraiser's office.

The Verandah does not appear to carry a Community Development District (CDD) assessment — but verify this directly during your due diligence. CDDs are common in master-planned Florida communities and can add several hundred to several thousand dollars annually to your ownership costs. Our complete guide to CDD fees in Southwest Florida is required reading before you buy in any new or recently developed community.

On flood risk: The Verandah sits at approximately seven feet of elevation in the Buckingham area, which is inland from the coast. Many homes here carry lower flood zone classifications than waterfront or coastal properties — but you should always verify the specific parcel's FEMA flood zone designation during due diligence and get a flood elevation certificate if the property is in or near a Zone AE. Your lender will require this if flood insurance is triggered.

Who Lives Here and Who It's Best For

The Verandah is an all-ages, all-welcome community — there are no age restrictions. In practice, the median age skews older (Census data puts the median at 67, with a majority of residents over 65), reflecting the lifestyle appeal for active adults and retirees. But it's not a 55-plus community, and families do live here.

The community draws buyers who want resort-quality amenities, a natural Florida setting, and optional golf without the financial commitment of a mandatory membership. It's particularly popular with:

  • Retirees from the Northeast and Midwest relocating to Southwest Florida
  • Active adults who want resort amenities and nature access without full-time country club overhead
  • Golfers who want two private courses with flexibility on membership structure
  • Buyers who want newer construction (2003 and forward) in East Fort Myers

If you're comparing The Verandah to communities like River Hall or Gateway, the biggest differentiators are the riverfront setting, the two-course layout, the optional golf structure, and the sheer amount of preserve land that surrounds you. River Hall, by contrast, offers a more compact layout with a mandatory bundled golf membership — a different cost structure and a different daily feel.

What's Nearby

The Shops at Verandah on Palm Beach Boulevard has a Publix and supporting retail — you won't need to drive far for everyday needs. The broader SR-80 corridor connects you to Colonial Boulevard and the larger Fort Myers commercial grid relatively quickly.

A few reference points:

  • Manatee Park: ~5 minutes
  • Lee Memorial Hospital (East): ~15 minutes
  • Downtown Fort Myers / River District: ~20 minutes
  • Edison & Ford Winter Estates: ~20 minutes
  • Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW): ~25–30 minutes via I-75

Schools in this area fall under the Lee County School District. The local high school zone is Riverdale High School.

Before You Schedule a Tour

A few things worth doing before you visit a model home or call the builder's sales office:

First, get pre-approved if you're financing. The Verandah's price range and the HOA documentation review both go more smoothly when your financial picture is clear upfront.

Second, decide how important golf is to you. If you're a serious golfer, schedule a round or a course tour — the two courses have meaningfully different characters, and you'll want to play both before committing. If golf is a bonus but not a requirement, make sure the non-golf amenities check every box you need.

Third, talk to a buyer's agent who knows this community and the East Fort Myers market. The builder's sales team represents the builder — not you. Having your own representation costs you nothing (seller and builder pay the commission) and means someone is actually reviewing the purchase agreement and HOA documents on your behalf before you sign.

Every buyer's situation is different — your timeline, your financing, your non-negotiables. The only way to know if The Verandah is the right fit is to walk it with someone who knows what to look for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is golf membership mandatory at The Verandah in Fort Myers?

No. Golf membership at The Verandah is optional — not bundled with home purchase and not mandatory. Residents can choose from various membership tiers through the Verandah Golf Club, or live in the community and never set foot on the course. This makes The Verandah more accessible to non-golfers than communities with mandatory or bundled memberships.

What are the HOA fees at The Verandah Fort Myers?

HOA fees at The Verandah typically range from approximately $639 to $900 per month depending on the neighborhood and home type. Fees cover gated security, community amenities, and common area maintenance. Always request the current HOA disclosure documents and reserve fund budget during your due diligence period — Florida law requires sellers to provide these before closing.

What home types are available at The Verandah in Fort Myers?

The Verandah offers coach homes (approximately 1,488–2,040 sq ft), attached villas (1,641–2,206 sq ft), and single-family homes ranging from roughly 1,831 to over 5,600 square feet across 20-plus distinct neighborhoods. Neal Communities is the current active builder, with new construction starting from the upper $200s and quick move-in homes in the $380s–$630s. Resale inventory typically runs from the mid-$400s to over $1.2 million.

What are the two golf courses at The Verandah?

The Verandah features two 18-hole championship courses: Old Orange, designed by Bob Cupp, and Whispering Oak, co-designed by Jack Nicklaus and Jack Nicklaus II. Whispering Oak has been recognized among the top private courses in Florida by Golf Inc. Magazine. Both are managed through the Verandah Golf Club with optional resident membership.

How far is The Verandah from downtown Fort Myers and the airport?

The Verandah is on State Road 80 approximately two miles east of I-75 in the 33905 zip code. Downtown Fort Myers is about 10 miles to the west — roughly 15–20 minutes. Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW) is accessible via I-75 south and typically runs 25–30 minutes depending on traffic.
