Wildcat Run in Estero, FL: what buyers need to know in 2026

By Mark & Dawn Borg·September 15, 2026·6 min read

Wildcat Run is a small, intimate gated golf community in the Corkscrew area of Estero — only 450 homes on 514 acres, built around an Arnold Palmer-designed, par-72 championship course established in 1985 and since renovated. What sets it apart from most private clubs in the area: no waitlist for golf membership. The 26,000-square-foot clubhouse carries two dining venues, a pro shop, a fitness and wellness center, and five lighted Har-Tru tennis courts. Condos generally run $230,000 to $599,000, with single-family homes spanning roughly $425,000 to $2.3 million.

Buyers who golf regularly and have shopped other Naples and Estero clubs usually react to Wildcat Run the same way: relief. Waitlists of a year or more are common at bundled-golf communities in this market. Wildcat Run doesn't have one.

An Arnold Palmer course without the wait

Wildcat Run's 18-hole course plays to a par of 72 across roughly 7,021 yards, with wide, rolling fairways set against palm trees and cypress preserves — a genuinely picturesque round for players at every skill level. The course includes practice facilities and a driving range with four target greens. What makes it stand out isn't just the design pedigree — it's access. Many private clubs in Naples and Estero maintain long membership waitlists; Wildcat Run doesn't, which is a real, practical advantage for buyers who want to be playing within weeks of closing rather than a year or more later.

We've had buyers walk away from otherwise-perfect homes in other clubs purely over the golf waitlist. At Wildcat Run, that objection simply doesn't come up.

A clubhouse built for daily use, not just special occasions

At 26,000 square feet, Wildcat Run's clubhouse includes a formal dining room and a more casual Grill Room with an outdoor dining terrace, a pro shop, locker rooms, a fitness and wellness center, and five lighted Har-Tru tennis courts just steps from the clubhouse. It's a genuinely complete amenity package for a community of only 450 homes — most communities this size carry a fraction of the facilities.

What to know before you buy

Small community, wide price range

With only 450 homes, Wildcat Run's inventory ranges from condos in the low $200,000s up to custom estates over $2 million — a wider spread than many similarly sized communities, which means the right fit depends heavily on which section and property type you're shopping. Large, private lots are common throughout, which is unusual for a community of this scale.

Flood zone

Much of Wildcat Run sits in Flood Zone X, which typically means flood insurance isn't required for a financed purchase — confirm the specific designation for the lot you're considering, since it can vary within the community. Our guide to flood insurance costs in Southwest Florida covers how this affects your overall budget.

Location

Wildcat Run sits in the Corkscrew area of Estero, minutes from Miromar Outlets, Coconut Point, Hertz Arena, Florida Gulf Coast University, I-75, and Southwest Florida International Airport — centrally located between Naples and Fort Myers.
