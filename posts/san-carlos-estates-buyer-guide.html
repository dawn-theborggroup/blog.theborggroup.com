San Carlos Estates, Bonita Springs: what buyers need to know about this no-HOA acreage community

By Mark & Dawn Borg·September 15, 2026·7 min read

San Carlos Estates is one of the most genuinely distinctive communities in Bonita Springs — a neighborhood that sits entirely within city limits yet feels nothing like the manicured gated communities that define most of Southwest Florida. Lots run approximately 1.25 acres, the roads are private, and AG-2 zoning creates a level of personal freedom that simply doesn't exist anywhere else in the surrounding area: no HOA, no CDD, and no architectural review committee standing between you and a boat, an RV, a workshop, or a few horses.

Most buyers who find San Carlos Estates were looking for something specific — a large lot, no HOA, room for a boat or RV, or the ability to keep animals — and discovering it exists within Bonita Springs city limits is often a genuine surprise. The community spans a range of home styles and price points, from classic older Florida-style ranch homes to newly constructed estate residences priced at $2 million and above.

What AG-2 zoning actually means for buyers

AG-2 is an agricultural residential zoning designation that applies throughout San Carlos Estates and nowhere else in this part of Bonita Springs — it's the foundation of everything that makes the community different.

  • Boats, RVs & trailers — store them directly on your property with no approval required. On a 1.25-acre lot there's room for a large vessel, an RV, and equipment simultaneously.
  • Animals & agricultural use — horses, goats, chickens, and other animals are permitted on appropriately sized lots, along with light agricultural or hobby-farm activity — categorically unavailable in any gated community in the area.
  • Build without approval committees — a detached garage, workshop, guest cottage, or pole barn goes through the City of Bonita Springs permitting office directly, not an HOA architectural review board.

Private roads and the tax assessment

All roads within San Carlos Estates are private — not maintained by the city or Lee County. Instead, road and drainage infrastructure is funded through an annual assessment that appears on each property's tax bill. This is not an HOA fee: no community rules, no architectural review process, no rental restrictions, and no association governance whatsoever. Think of it as a utility assessment for the infrastructure serving your property — the exact amount varies and should be confirmed on the current tax record for any specific property you're considering.

No HOA means no monthly fees, no architectural review board, and no rules about boats, RVs, or commercial vehicles on your property. For the right buyer, that freedom is worth more than any amenity package.

Flood Zone X — a real financial advantage

San Carlos Estates is designated FEMA Flood Zone X, outside the high-risk flood area — flood insurance isn't required for federally backed mortgages, and for buyers who carry it anyway, premiums run substantially lower than in high-risk zones. In a region where flood insurance can run from several hundred to several thousand dollars a year, that's a meaningful differentiator both for annual carrying costs and the eventual resale conversation.

Types of homes and price ranges

  • Classic Florida ranch ($700K–$1.2M): original ranch homes on ~1.25-acre lots — solid construction, established landscaping, full benefit of AG-2 zoning, many with pools and outbuildings
  • Updated & modern estates ($1.2M–$2M): renovated or newer-build homes with modern interiors, impact windows and doors, resort-quality pools, and updated systems
  • New construction luxury ($2M–$4M+): custom-built estate homes taking full advantage of AG-2 zoning — expansive floor plans, three-car garages, resort pools and spas, and detached guest houses

Location

San Carlos Estates sits east of US-41, bordered by Pelican Landing to the north and Bonita Bay to the west — within Bonita Springs city limits, approximately 8 miles (about 15 minutes) from Bonita Beach and Barefoot Beach Preserve, minutes from Coconut Point Mall, and roughly 20 minutes from RSW International Airport. Acreage living without trading away access to daily life.
