Verdana Village vs. Kingston in Estero: which should you choose?

By Mark & Dawn Borg·October 5, 2026·8 min read

Should you buy in Verdana Village or Kingston? Verdana Village is the proven choice: it's already built out in large part, has residents, an amenity campus, and published HOA and CDD numbers. Kingston is the early-buyer choice: a much larger community along the same corridor with more builders, but with homes, pricing, and fees still being finalized. If you want to know exactly what you're buying today, lean Verdana. If you're comfortable waiting and want first pick of a brand-new community, Kingston is worth watching.

Both sit on Corkscrew Road in Estero, and both come from the same developer, Cameratta Companies. That's where the similarities end. Here's how we help buyers sort them out.

How are Verdana Village and Kingston different?

Verdana Village is a 2,138-acre community with roughly 2,400 planned homes, built by Lennar and Pulte. More than 1,200 acres are preserved as natural land, there are 31 lakes, and The Shoppes at Verdana Village, with a Publix, sits just outside the gates. The signature amenity is the Indoor Sports Complex with four indoor pickleball courts, two tennis courts, and a basketball court. Call us for current pricing and availability. Read the details in our Verdana Village guide.

Kingston is closer to a small city than a single neighborhood. It covers more than 7,000 acres and is approved for up to about 10,000 homes, with more than 3,300 acres set aside as preserve and wildlife habitat and about 700,000 square feet of planned commercial space. Kingston South is planned for about 3,625 homes from Lennar, Pulte, and Neal Communities, and Esplanade at Kingston is Taylor Morrison's section with about 1,285 homes. Kolter Homes is also part of the builder mix. Our Kingston buyer's guide has the full structure.

Which one can you move into sooner?

Verdana Village, by a wide margin. It has residents today, finished homes, and Quick Move-In inventory from Pulte. Kingston is just getting started. Groundbreaking was in November 2025, model homes open for touring on October 17, 2026, and first closings and move-ins are expected in early 2027.

How do the costs compare?

This is where the gap is widest. Verdana's numbers are documented: HOA fees of about $342 to $393 a month depending on the home series, annual CDD fees of roughly $1,872 to $2,218, and a $480 annual food and beverage minimum. Kingston hasn't published final HOA or CDD figures. Communities of this scale in Lee County nearly always carry CDD assessments, and comparable communities in Estero and Bonita Springs typically run about $1,500 to $5,000 or more a year, but treat that as a planning range, not Kingston's number. Ask for the projected assessment on your specific homesite in writing before you sign. Our CDD guide shows you how to read it.

On price, Kingston's final pricing by builder and section is still being released, and Verdana's current pricing changes with builder inventory and incentives. Call us for current numbers on both, and compare the full monthly cost rather than the starting price.

Which one fits you better?

  • Choose Verdana Village if you want indoor pickleball, a community you can walk through today, firm fee numbers, and the option of new construction or resale.
  • Choose Kingston if you want a brand-new community, a wider choice of builders, and you're comfortable with a longer wait and open questions on fees and amenities.
  • Wait on Kingston if your budget depends on exact monthly costs. Until HOA and CDD figures exist, you're comparing a known number to a guess.

With a deposit on a not-yet-built home, the risk isn't just price. It's the gap between signing and closing, when rates, your finances, and the builder's schedule can all move. If you go the Kingston route, understand your deposit protections and exit conditions first. And register an independent buyer's agent on your very first visit to any sales center. The builder's team represents the builder.

The right call depends on your timeline, your budget, and how much uncertainty you're comfortable carrying. We'll walk through both with you side by side. Common questions

Should I buy in Verdana Village or Kingston?

Buy in Verdana Village if you want a proven community with residents, an amenity campus, and published HOA and CDD figures. Consider Kingston if you want a brand-new community with several builders and can wait for homes, pricing, and fees to be finalized. Your timeline and tolerance for uncertainty decide it.

Is Kingston the same developer as Verdana Village?

Yes. Both communities come from Cameratta Companies and sit along Corkscrew Road in Estero. They differ in scale, builder mix, and how far along they are.

Who builds homes in Kingston?

Kingston's builders include Lennar, Pulte Homes, Neal Communities, Taylor Morrison, and Kolter Homes. Taylor Morrison's section is called Esplanade at Kingston. Which builders are in which section, and at what prices, is still being finalized.

When can I move into Kingston?

Model homes open for touring on October 17, 2026, and first closings and move-ins are expected in early 2027. Schedules for new communities can shift, so confirm current timing with the builders before you commit.

Will Kingston have CDD fees like Verdana Village?

Final figures haven't been published, but large new master-planned communities in Lee County almost always carry CDD assessments. Get the projected assessment for your specific homesite in writing before you sign a contract.
