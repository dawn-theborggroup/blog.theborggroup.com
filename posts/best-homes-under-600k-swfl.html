Buyer Tips

Best under $600K homes in Cape Coral, Fort Myers, and Estero: top picks for today's market

By Mark & Dawn Borg·February 4, 2026·8 min read

Discover some of the best value homes currently available under $600,000 in Cape Coral, Fort Myers, and Estero, with local insight to help you make a confident, informed decision in today's Southwest Florida market.

While prices have adjusted from recent highs, well-positioned homes under $600K remain in strong demand. Knowing where the best opportunities are, and why they stand out, can make all the difference.

Cape Coral: a boater's paradise with waterfront value

Cape Coral remains one of the region's best values for buyers who want water access without the premium of a coastal-front address. What stands out under $600K:

Waterfront access: Many homes in this price range still offer Gulf-access or freshwater canal frontage. Neighborhood variety: From established Southeast Cape Coral to newer Southwest areas, buyers can choose between charm and modern construction. Strong investment fundamentals: Continued infrastructure improvements and buyer demand support long-term appreciation.

Homes here often blend mid-century Florida architecture with updated interiors, making them especially attractive to buyers seeking value without sacrificing lifestyle.

Fort Myers: historic character meets everyday convenience

Fort Myers offers a unique mix of historic neighborhoods, riverfront living, and modern redevelopment, appealing to buyers who want culture, walkability, and convenience. What stands out in Fort Myers:

Historic neighborhoods: Areas like Edison Park and Dean Park feature mature trees and distinctive architecture. Downtown energy: The River District provides dining, events, and entertainment close to home. Central location: Easy access to beaches, medical facilities, golf, and RSW Airport.

Under $600K, buyers can still find single-family homes and condos that balance character with updated features, ideal for retirees, professionals, and seasonal residents.

Estero: modern communities with a suburban lifestyle

Estero offers a central location between Naples and Fort Myers. Why Estero works for today's buyers:

Resort-style living: Communities like Bella Terra and Corkscrew Shores offer pools, fitness centers, and social amenities. Convenient access: Close to I-75, Coconut Point Mall, and Southwest Florida International Airport. Newer construction options: Many homes under $600K feature open floor plans, impact windows, and energy-efficient design.

Estero is especially appealing for buyers seeking newer homes, strong HOA management, and a lock-and-leave lifestyle.

Working with a local expert who understands micro-markets across Cape Coral, Fort Myers, and Estero can help you identify opportunities before they're widely recognized.

Key considerations when buying under $600K in Southwest Florida

Before choosing a home, it's important to look beyond the list price and evaluate how each area aligns with your goals: Lifestyle priorities — waterfront access, gated communities, schools, or proximity to shopping and beaches. Market dynamics — inventory levels, days on market, and negotiation flexibility vary by neighborhood. Long-term value — consider insurance costs, HOA fees, rental potential, and resale appeal.
