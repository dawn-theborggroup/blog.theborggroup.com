Seller Tips

Is a cash offer or traditional sale better in Southwest Florida?

By Mark & Dawn Borg·January 11, 2026·3 min read

Cash offers are common in Southwest Florida, but they're not always the best choice.

Cash sales offer speed, but often at a lower price. Traditional listings may take longer but frequently yield stronger net proceeds.

The Borg Group helps sellers compare net outcomes, not just headlines.

What "speed vs. price" actually looks like

A cash sale can often close in one to two weeks, versus the 30-45 days typical of a financed transaction — but that speed usually comes at a discount to full market value, and how large a discount varies enormously depending on who's making the offer. A qualified individual buyer competing on the open market can land close to full value; an unsolicited investor or wholesaler pitch is often priced well below what the home would fetch with proper marketing. If you've received an unsolicited cash offer, ask for proof of funds and a track record before you compare it against what listing on the open market would likely bring.

For sellers with real time pressure — a relocation, a probate timeline, a home needing more repair than they want to fund — a fast cash close often makes sense.
