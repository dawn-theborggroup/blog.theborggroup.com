Esplanade Lake Club Fort Myers FL: what buyers need to know before making an offer in 2026

By Mark & Dawn Borg·September 18, 2026·10 min read

Is Esplanade Lake Club in Fort Myers Worth Buying Into in 2026?

Esplanade Lake Club is a gated, all-ages lakefront community in Fort Myers, FL (33913), developed by Taylor Morrison and Pulte on 778 acres surrounding a 700-acre boatable freshwater lake system. There are no age restrictions. HOA fees run $307–$607 per month depending on home type, and the community carries a CDD (Community Development District) assessment that appears separately on your Lee County property tax bill. Homes range from the low $400s (condos and resale villas) to $3.75M+ for lakefront estate properties. With approximately 27 active listings and an average of 101 days on market as of September 2026, buyers have meaningful negotiating leverage — if they come prepared.

If you've been researching waterfront communities in Fort Myers, Esplanade Lake Club keeps coming up. And for good reason: it's one of the few places in Southwest Florida where you can own a home with access to a 700-acre boatable freshwater lake system — and still be 10 minutes from RSW Airport and 30 minutes from the beach.

But there's a lot of noise out there. Some sites list it as a 55+ community. Others show HOA fees that vary wildly from one listing to the next. And if you're planning to buy new construction here, the builder contract process works very differently from what most buyers expect.

Here's what you actually need to know before you schedule a tour or sign anything.

The Community: What Makes Esplanade Lake Club Different

Esplanade Lake Club sits off Alico Road in Fort Myers, east of Ben Hill Griffin Parkway — a location that puts you in the middle of one of Lee County's most convenient corridors. Grocery stores, restaurants, Coconut Point Mall, and Gulf Coast Town Center are within a few miles. You're about 10 minutes from Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW) and roughly 30 minutes to Bonita Beach or Fort Myers Beach.

The community's signature feature is Lake Como, a 352-acre lake that connects to the broader Miromar Lakes water system for approximately 700 total acres of accessible freshwater. That's not just a retention pond you look at — residents can use personal watercraft, bring motorized boats to the community's boat ramp, reserve dry boat storage, and some homesites include private boat docks. If you've been drawn to the idea of lakefront living without the entry cost and mandatory club structure of a place like Miromar Lakes, Esplanade Lake Club deserves a serious look.

On the age restriction question: Esplanade Lake Club shows up on 55+ real estate listing sites, and the Taylor Morrison branding leans into "active lifestyle" language. But the community carries no formal age restriction — it's an all-ages gated community. If you're in your 40s or 50s and felt like this community wasn't for you, that assumption is worth revisiting. If you're in your 60s or 70s and want neighbors who share your lifestyle orientation, you'll find plenty of that here too. The community appeals broadly because of what it offers, not because of who's required to live there.

Development at Esplanade Lake Club began in 2019 and continues as of 2026. With approximately 650 homes planned across 778 acres, there's a mix of completed new construction, move-in ready inventory from builders, and early resale homes from original owners.

Homes and Pricing: What You'll Pay and What You'll Get

Three builders are active or have been active at Esplanade Lake Club: Taylor Morrison (the primary developer), Pulte Homes, and Seagate Development. Each brings different floor plans, pricing structures, and contract terms — which matters for how you approach negotiation.

Home types and sizes:

  • Attached twin villas: 2–3 bedrooms, 1,543–1,579 sq ft (some floor plan series now sold out)
  • Single-family homes: 2–5 bedrooms, 1,543–4,123 sq ft across 13 floor plans
  • Estate/lakefront homes: Up to 4,896 sq ft; listed into the $3M+ range for premier waterfront lots
  • Condos: Resale units in the $399K–$430K range

Current pricing (September 2026):

  • New construction starting price: approximately $589,999
  • Condo resale average: ~$428K
  • Single-family home average: ~$1.29M
  • Overall median list price: ~$822K
  • Full price range across all listings: $399,000–$3,750,000

Market conditions right now: With approximately 27 active listings and an average DOM of 101 days, this is a buyer's market. Homes are sitting longer. Price reductions are happening across Lee County. If you're shopping in the $500K–$900K range, you have real negotiating room — especially on resale homes where sellers don't have the builder's incentive structure to compete with.

HOA and CDD fees: HOA fees run $307–$607 per month, depending on home type. Villas run on the lower end; larger single-family and lakefront homes are on the higher end. The community also has a CDD (Community Development District), which means a separate non-ad valorem assessment appears on your annual Lee County property tax bill alongside your regular taxes. CDDs fund the infrastructure bonds — roads, utilities, community improvements — and are distinct from HOA fees. Before writing any offer, request the specific parcel's CDD annual assessment and outstanding bond balance. If you're not familiar with how CDDs work, our guide to CDD fees in Southwest Florida covers what to ask, what to watch for, and how to calculate your true annual cost of ownership.

Property taxes: Based on recent data, the average annual property tax for homes at Esplanade Lake Club runs approximately $11,700. Keep in mind that assessed value resets to your purchase price the year after you close — so if you're buying at a price lower than a prior comparable sale, your ongoing tax burden may be lower than what the current owner is paying. Applying for Florida's homestead exemption ($25,000–$50,000 reduction in taxable value) can save you $400–$550 per year and also activates Save Our Homes, which caps future assessed-value increases at 3% or CPI, whichever is lower.

Amenities and Lifestyle: Who This Community Is Built For

The amenity package at Esplanade Lake Club is resort-caliber for the price point. Here's what's included in your HOA fees:

  • Bahama Bar & Grille — waterfront on-site dining, casual atmosphere
  • Koquina Sand Spa — full-service spa offering massage, skincare, and wellness treatments
  • Resort-style pool with resistance lap lanes
  • Private beach area within the community
  • Boat dock, boat ramps, and dry boat storage — direct access to Lake Como
  • Kayak and paddleboard launch
  • Lighted tennis courts
  • Pickleball courts
  • Bocce ball courts
  • State-of-the-art fitness center
  • Walking and biking trails throughout the community
  • Pet park with grooming stations
  • Full-time Lifestyle Manager programming events, classes, and activities

This setup resonates most with buyers who want a resort-lifestyle community without paying for an equity golf club membership (there's no golf here — and for many buyers, that's exactly the right trade-off). It also attracts buyers who want real water access, not just a view over a retention pond.

Buyers relocating from the Northeast and Midwest — particularly from Ohio, Michigan, New York, and New Jersey — consistently respond well to this combination: gated security, on-site dining, full amenity access, and genuine waterfront activity in a price range that feels like value when you're comparing it to what similar access costs in coastal markets back home.

If mandatory golf, a full equity club membership, or a more exclusive community scale is important to you, compare this community against Miromar Lakes Beach & Golf Club (higher price floor, private golf and beach club, more exclusivity) or WildBlue in Estero (similar boatable-lake concept, Estero location, broadly comparable price range).

The Buying Process: What to Do Before You Visit the Sales Center

This section matters more than most buyers realize — especially for new construction.

Register your buyer's agent before your first visit

Taylor Morrison — like most new home builders in Southwest Florida — requires that your buyer's agent register you with the sales center before your first visit to the property. If you walk into the sales center without a pre-registered agent and later try to bring one in, you may permanently lose your right to independent representation at no additional cost to you. The builder's on-site agent represents the builder, not you. They cannot negotiate on your behalf, advise you objectively on contract terms, or help you compare the cost of upgrades versus resale alternatives. Before you do anything else, call a buyer's agent and ask them to register you.

Builder contracts are not FAR-BAR contracts

When you buy from Taylor Morrison or Pulte, you sign the builder's proprietary purchase agreement — not the Florida Association of Realtors FAR-BAR As-Is contract that governs resale transactions. Builder contracts typically include:

  • Design center add-ons that can run $50,000–$150,000+ over the base price
  • Extended closing timelines (10–18 months for new builds, or less for quick-move-in inventory)
  • Deposit terms that favor the builder, with limited contingency protections
  • Escalation provisions tied to material costs in some contracts

A buyer's agent with new construction experience can walk you through what's negotiable and what isn't before you sign. On a purchase of this size, that conversation is worth having.

Preferred lender incentives — and how to evaluate them

Both Taylor Morrison and Pulte offer closing cost credits when you use their preferred lending partner. These credits can be substantial — sometimes $10,000–$20,000 or more depending on the purchase price and current market incentives. You're never required to use the builder's preferred lender, but the math sometimes favors it. The right move is to get a quote from the builder's lender and from your own lender, then compare total costs including interest rate, points, and any incentives. Your buyer's agent can help you model both scenarios side by side.

Incentive timing works in your favor right now

Builders price new inventory in phases, and incentive packages — upgrades, rate buydowns, closing cost credits — tend to be most aggressive at the end of a phase or during slower sales periods. September 2026 is currently a slower period across Lee County new construction. If you're buying new here, this is a more favorable moment than you might find six months from now when snowbird season drives more activity into the market.

For resale buyers: the due diligence checklist

Esplanade Lake Club resale transactions use the standard Florida FAR-BAR contract. Before you finalize an offer, request and review:

  • HOA disclosure package and financials — budget, reserve study, any pending special assessments
  • HOA estoppel letter — confirms current fees, any outstanding balance on the unit, and any violations
  • CDD parcel balance and annual assessment — exact amount and remaining bond life
  • Seller's Property Disclosure (SPDR) — Florida's required disclosure form covering known defects, flood history, and material issues
  • Flood elevation certificate — verify the flood zone designation for the specific property. While much of the community sits in FEMA Zone X (lower risk), waterfront and lake-adjacent lots can carry different designations. Check LeePA.org and FEMA's flood map service for the specific parcel before committing.

In Lee County, the seller customarily pays for the owner's title insurance policy — unlike Collier County, where buyers typically pay. Your title company will walk you through the closing costs, doc stamps ($0.70 per $100 of sale price, paid by the seller), and any HOA transfer fees or capital contribution charges due at closing.

How Esplanade Lake Club Compares

Esplanade Lake Club vs. Miromar Lakes: Miromar Lakes is the luxury benchmark in this corridor — private golf, a private beach club on a 700-acre lake system, and a more exclusive community feel. Miromar's homes typically start well above $1M, and the lifestyle demands a higher overall cost of ownership. Esplanade Lake Club gives you access to the same lake system (Lake Como connects to Miromar's waters) and resort amenities at a significantly more accessible entry point, starting in the $400s for condos and villas.

Esplanade Lake Club vs. WildBlue (Estero): WildBlue is the closest conceptual match — boatable freshwater lake, resort amenities, newer construction, gated community. WildBlue is located in Estero off Corkscrew Road (I-75 corridor) rather than Fort Myers off Alico Road. WildBlue's lake is approximately 225 acres, smaller than Lake Como. Price ranges and HOA fees are broadly comparable between the two. Choosing between them often comes down to location preference, which specific floor plans suit your needs, and which community's activity programming feels like a better fit. Both are worth touring before you decide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Esplanade Lake Club a 55+ community?

No — Esplanade Lake Club has no formal age restriction. The community is open to buyers of all ages. It's listed on some active adult and 55+ real estate sites because of its lifestyle orientation and marketing, but this is not a legal restriction. You do not need to be 55 or older to purchase here, and younger buyers are welcome.

What are the HOA fees at Esplanade Lake Club in Fort Myers?

HOA fees at Esplanade Lake Club range from approximately $307 to $607 per month, depending on your home type. Villas and smaller floor plans tend to be on the lower end; larger single-family homes and lakefront lots are higher. These fees typically cover common area maintenance, amenity access, landscaping of community areas, and management costs. Always request the current HOA financials and reserve study before closing.

Does Esplanade Lake Club have CDD fees?

Yes — Esplanade Lake Club operates within a Community Development District (CDD). The CDD assessment appears as a separate non-ad valorem line on your annual Lee County property tax bill, distinct from HOA fees. CDD fees fund community infrastructure bonds. Request the specific parcel's CDD annual assessment amount and remaining bond balance for any property you're considering before submitting an offer.

Can I use a motorized boat on the lake at Esplanade Lake Club?

Yes — Lake Como at Esplanade Lake Club permits motorized watercraft. The community provides boat ramps, dry storage facilities, and some homesites include private boat docks. Specific rules on motor size, speed limits, and permitted hours are governed by the HOA. Request the current community rules and regulations document as part of your standard due diligence before closing.

Do I need a buyer's agent to purchase new construction at Esplanade Lake Club?

You're not legally required to use a buyer's agent, but it's strongly in your interest to have one. The builder's on-site sales representative works for the builder — not for you — and cannot negotiate on your behalf or advise you objectively. Critically, Taylor Morrison requires buyer registration before your first visit to the sales center. If you walk in unrepresented first, you may forfeit your right to independent representation at no cost to you later in the process.

Esplanade Lake Club gives you one of the most compelling combinations in the Fort Myers market right now: genuine boatable-lake access, resort amenities you'll actually use, and a price range that starts meaningfully below the luxury tier.
