Valencia Bonita, Bonita Springs: complete 55+ buyer guide for 2026

By Mark & Dawn Borg·September 4, 2026·8 min read

Valencia Bonita is a gated, 55+ active adult community built by GL Homes in Bonita Springs, Florida. Located on 347 acres with 997 single-family homes and villas, the community features a 45,000-square-foot waterfront clubhouse, 20+ floor plan options across five collections, and no CDD fees.

The closest comparable community many buyers also look at is Pelican Landing — which is all-ages, golf-optional, and offers beach club access on Big Hickory Island — but Valencia Bonita wins on amenity density and the no-CDD advantage for buyers focused on total monthly cost.

Homes, collections & current pricing

GL Homes organized Valencia Bonita into five collections, each targeting a different size range and lifestyle: Signature Villas — 1,738-2,024 square feet, 2 bedrooms + den, 2-car garage, the entry point for villa living in the community. Palazzo Villas — larger paired villa footprints with upgraded standard features and finishes. Classic Residences — single-family homes from approximately 1,900 to 2,754 square feet, the most popular collection at Valencia Bonita. Grand Collection — single-family homes in the 2,700-3,100 square foot range with premium lot placement options. Estate Collection — the largest single-family homes, up to approximately 3,350 square feet, typically on oversized lots.

As of September 2026, the active resale market shows 22 listings priced from roughly $520,000 to $1.25 million, with a median list price around $754,611. Days on market average 106 — notably higher than the overall Bonita Springs market median of 65 days. That tells you buyers are taking their time on these decisions and negotiating carefully, rather than rushing.

For new construction from GL Homes, pricing shifts with available inventory and builder incentive programs. The builder periodically offers closing cost credits, design center upgrade allowances, or rate buydown programs, particularly on completed spec homes sitting in inventory. Those incentives are real, but they're only available if you know what to ask for.

The broader Bonita Springs market has seen inventory contract significantly in 2026 — down roughly 28% year-over-year — while pending sales have climbed approximately 37%. Buyer demand within Valencia Bonita's price corridor is steady, driven heavily by relocators from Texas and California who are comparing multiple communities before committing.

HOA fees, and the CDD fee you won't pay

This is where Valencia Bonita separates itself from a lot of Southwest Florida communities. There are no CDD fees at Valencia Bonita. In communities like Fiddler's Creek, many Esplanade developments, and newer builds along the I-75 corridor, CDD fees can add $1,000 to $4,000+ per year to your property tax bill — Florida's mechanism for letting developers recover infrastructure costs (roads, utilities, drainage) over a 20-30 year period. Valencia Bonita was structured without a CDD, which means that long-term cost doesn't follow you into your retirement budget.

Amenities

The 45,000-square-foot clubhouse anchors a full amenity package: 8 lighted tennis courts, 8 pickleball courts, bocce courts, a putting green, two dog parks, a resort pool and lap pool, a resistance pool, and walking paths.

Zest! Restaurant operates inside the clubhouse — a full-service dining option without leaving the community. Most competing active adult communities have café-style grab-and-go options; Zest! is a sit-down restaurant with a full menu. For buyers who want to reduce their daily car dependency or simply enjoy a built-in social dining environment, this is a genuine differentiator.

If you haven't visited yet, GL Homes offers the Stay and Play program — three nights of on-site accommodations for approximately $99, with full access to community amenities. It's one of the most useful tools prospective buyers have for experiencing what daily life at Valencia Bonita actually feels like before committing. Ask the sales team about availability when you schedule your first appointment.

The most important thing 55+ buyers get wrong

Here's what the brochure won't tell you, and what we tell every client before they step into a GL Homes sales center: your buyer's agent must be registered before your first visit.

GL Homes — like most large national builders — has a buyer's agent pre-registration policy. If you walk into the sales center unrepresented, or if your agent isn't pre-registered before your initial visit, GL Homes' contract language does not obligate them to honor broker co-op compensation for your agent later in the process. Some builders interpret even a casual first contact — picking up a brochure, doing a quick walk-through — as establishing a direct buyer relationship that precludes agent representation afterward.

Why does this matter to you as a buyer? The builder's sales team represents GL Homes, not you. They're trained to tell the community's value story and protect the builder's interests — that's their job, and they do it well. Your agent's job is different: reviewing the builder contract for clauses that don't serve you, identifying what's actually negotiable on spec home pricing, pushing for closing cost credits or upgrade allowances, and coordinating the inspection and closing process in your favor.

Builder contracts are not standard Florida Realtors forms — they're drafted by the builder's legal team. An experienced buyer's agent who has closed multiple GL Homes transactions knows which clauses need attention, what the current incentive landscape looks like, and where the real negotiating leverage sits.

Before you schedule your first tour of Valencia Bonita, contact your agent and have them register with the sales center directly. It takes a few minutes and preserves your full representation rights for the entire purchase process.
