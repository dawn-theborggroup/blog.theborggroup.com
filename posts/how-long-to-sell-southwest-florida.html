How long does it take to sell a home in Southwest Florida?

By Mark & Dawn Borg·April 23, 2026·7 min read

In today's Southwest Florida market, most well-priced homes are going under contract in 30 to 75 days, though your timeline depends heavily on price, condition, and location.

What "days on market" really means for you as a seller

Days on Market (DOM) tracks how long your home sits active before receiving a contract. It's more than just a number, it directly impacts your leverage in negotiations, buyer perception of your home, and your financial timeline and moving plans. Homes that generate strong activity in the first 2-3 weeks tend to sell faster and closer to asking price. Once a listing lingers, buyers start looking for reasons why.

2026 market reality: Fort Myers vs. Cape Coral

The market has shifted from the rapid pace of 2021-2022. Buyers now have more options, and they're more selective.

The market today rewards precision over guesswork.

The 4 factors that control how fast your home sells

1. Location. Waterfront, gated communities, and high-demand areas move faster, especially in growing corridors.

2. Condition. Move-in-ready homes win. Deferred maintenance means longer DOM and lower offers.

3. Access. If buyers can't easily see it, they won't buy it. Limited showing availability slows momentum.

4. Price. This is the biggest lever. Even a 5% overpricing can double your time on market.

Key Southwest Florida-specific factors

Flood zone & insurance: Buyers are factoring in total cost. Homes in higher-risk zones need sharper pricing and transparency. New construction competition: Builders are offering incentives; resale homes must compete on value and presentation. Seasonality: Peak season runs November-April; summer is slower unless priced aggressively.

How to sell faster (and smarter)

Here's what's working right now: price correctly from day one (based on the last 90 days, not outdated comps); maximize your first 14 days, since this is when your home gets the most exposure; invest in presentation (photos, paint, staging); study your competition before listing; and create urgency early with strong marketing and accessibility.

When to make a move if your home isn't selling

If you're approaching 30-45 days without strong activity, it's time to reassess: price positioning, condition and presentation, showing feedback, and buyer objections (insurance, layout, location). Waiting too long leads to stale listings, which almost always sell for less in the end.

Bottom line

The market today rewards precision over guesswork. Homes that are priced correctly, presented well, and easy to show are still selling quickly. Homes that miss the mark are sitting, and often chasing the market down.
