Shadow Wood at The Brooks, Estero FL: what buyers need to know before making an offer in 2026

By Mark & Dawn Borg·August 17, 2026·10 min read

Shadow Wood at The Brooks is a 1,481-home private golf community set within The Brooks, a 2,492-acre master-planned development at the Coconut Road and Three Oaks Parkway corridor in Estero, FL. It's the only community in The Brooks where golf membership is optional and non-equity, with 54 holes of championship golf spread across three courses redesigned by Rees Jones. There's no CDD fee, golf membership carries a waitlist, and home prices range from coach home condos in the $400s to custom estate homes above $4 million.

Shadow Wood at The Brooks is one of Southwest Florida's most recognized private golf communities, and one of the most misunderstood. Buyers come in expecting it to work like other gated communities and quickly discover it has its own rules: 34 distinct sub-neighborhoods, three membership tiers, a golf waitlist, and some homes that carry a transferable golf membership embedded in the deed. Getting those details wrong before you make an offer can cost you.

Homes and pricing in 2026

Coach homes and condos: entry point for the community, expect $400,000-$865,000 depending on size, location, and condition. These are ideal for buyers who want the Shadow Wood lifestyle without a high-maintenance single-family home. Single-family villas and attached homes: generally $600,000-$1,000,000, a larger living footprint than coach homes with some private outdoor space. Single-family homes: $750,000-$2,000,000, the bulk of the community's inventory. Views of lakes, golf courses, or preserves significantly affect price. Custom luxury estates: $1.5 million and up, with the highest sales in recent years reaching $4.125 million.

As of mid-2026, approximately 15 active listings are on the market with a median list price around $749,000, and an average of 149 days on market. That's meaningful context: buyers have time to conduct proper due diligence, and sellers are generally negotiable (homes are selling at approximately 5% below list price on average). This is a buyer-favorable market right now.

Getting those details wrong before you make an offer can cost you.

No CDD fee, but a layered HOA structure

One cost consideration many buyers overlook: Shadow Wood has no CDD fee. The community predates Florida's Community Development District structure, which means you won't see a separate CDD line item on your property tax bill. If you've been looking at Verdana Village or The Place at Corkscrew where CDD fees run $1,320-$3,500 per year, that's a meaningful difference when comparing total annual cost of ownership.

The master HOA runs approximately $1,000-$1,200 per quarter depending on your sub-neighborhood. Many sub-associations carry additional fees, particularly for coach home and condo buildings where exterior maintenance and building insurance are included. Before you make an offer, ask for the full fee stack: master HOA plus sub-association plus any applicable club membership.

Important: when you purchase at Shadow Wood, your property tax bill will reset based on the purchase price, not the seller's lower assessed value. Florida's Save Our Homes cap lifts at sale. On a $1,000,000 purchase in Lee County (effective rate approximately 1.0-1.2%), budget $10,000-$12,000 per year in property taxes, more than the seller may be paying.

Amenities and lifestyle: golf, tennis, and the coming indoor pickleball era

The centerpiece of Shadow Wood is 54 holes of championship golf across three distinct courses (North, South, and Preserve) redesigned by Rees Jones. Golf membership is optional and non-equity — some homes carry a transferable golf membership that conveys to the buyer at purchase. Otherwise, you can join the waitlist or purchase a Lifestyle Membership, which includes tennis, bocce, dining, social events, and summer golf access.

The Commons Club: the master amenity hub

The Commons Club is the master amenity hub for all five communities in The Brooks. It includes a private Gulf beach club on Little Hickory Island, waterside dining at The Rookery, a wellness spa, pickleball, and social programming. Membership is optional for Shadow Wood residents and carries its own initiation fee and annual dues. Contact The Commons Club directly at (239) 949-3800 for current pricing.

Flood zone considerations

Most of Shadow Wood sits in FEMA Zone X, a minimal flood risk designation, due to its inland position east of US-41 along Coconut Road. Hurricane Ian caused wind damage in the community but no storm surge flooding. Individual parcels near lake edges may carry different designations, so request the flood elevation certificate for any property before making an offer.
