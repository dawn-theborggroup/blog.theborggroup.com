West Bay Club Estero FL: do you have to join the golf club to live there? 2026

By Mark & Dawn Borg·November 9, 2026·7 min read

No, you do not have to join the golf club to live at West Bay Club. Golf membership at this gated Estero community is optional, so you can buy a home and decide about the club separately. That makes West Bay Club different from bundled communities nearby, such as Pelican Sound, where club membership comes with every home. The trade-off is that you need to look at the club and the home as two separate decisions, each with its own costs and rules.

What is West Bay Club, and where is it?

West Bay Club is a gated community of about 868 acres in Estero, roughly a mile west of US 41 on Williams Road, with a staffed gatehouse at the entrance. It is bordered by the Estero River, Estero Bay and Halfway Creek, and about 500 acres of the property are wetlands and nature preserve. The golf course opened in the late 1990s, and the setting along the river and the bay is what most buyers notice first.

What kinds of homes are there?

West Bay Club has more variety than most Estero communities. You will find coach and carriage homes, villas, single-family homes, custom estate homes and high-rise condominiums, organized into separate neighborhoods. A few examples:

  • Turtle Pointe: two-story coach homes of roughly 1,600 to 2,040 square feet
  • Laurel Oaks and Riverbrooke: custom and estate homes, many 3,400 to 4,000 square feet and up
  • Jasmine Bay: two high-rise towers with residences from about 1,750 to more than 4,100 square feet
  • The Island at West Bay Club: a 24-story tower by Kolter Urban with 86 corner residences

Because the product types are so different, prices span a wide range, from condos and villas to estate homes and tower penthouses. Each neighborhood also has its own association and rules, so two homes a short walk apart can carry very different monthly costs.

How does the optional golf membership work?

The golf course is an 18-hole, par-72 Pete and P.B. Dye design that was renovated by Fry/Straka in 2018. Golf membership is offered in categories, including full golf and social options, and published sources describe the golf membership as capped. Initiation fees and annual dues are substantial and change from year to year, and the figures published online do not always agree, so we would not rely on any number you find on a website. Ask the club for its current membership schedule before you write an offer.

Optional membership matters for two kinds of buyers. If you do not play golf, you are not paying for a course you will not use. If you do play, you should confirm how membership is offered when you buy, whether there is a waiting list for your category, and what the costs will be after closing.

What about the beach and the water?

West Bay Club has a private beach club on Little Hickory Island, on Hickory Boulevard near Bonita Beach, which was rebuilt and reopened as a new facility. Inside the community, River Park on the Estero River has a boat launch and dry storage for boats up to about 40 feet, with kayak and canoe access into the preserve. For buyers who want the bay and the beach without living right on the Gulf, that combination is a big part of the appeal.

Being on the river and the bay also means you should check flood risk for the specific address, not the community as a whole. Our guide on how to check a home's flood risk in Estero explains what to look at.

At West Bay Club, the home and the club are two separate decisions. That gives you more control, but it also means the monthly picture is not in one number.

Who is West Bay Club a good fit for?

  • Buyers who want a gated Estero address with preserve and water views, but do not want a mandatory golf fee
  • Golfers who want a private course and are willing to look closely at membership terms
  • Boaters and kayakers who want river access from inside the community
  • Buyers who want a high-rise view or a single-family home in the same gated setting

How does it compare with bundled communities nearby?

In a bundled community, club membership is part of owning the home. Pelican Sound is a good example: its 2026 bundled fee is $11,495 a year, plus an initiation fee for first-time owners, and it applies to every home whether or not you play. Copperleaf at The Brooks is also bundled. That makes the cost easier to predict, but you pay for the club either way.

At West Bay Club, the cost depends on your choices: your neighborhood's association fees, the master association, and a club membership only if you want one. If you are deciding between the two models, our comparison of Grandezza and Wildcat Run shows how much club structure can change what you pay.

What should you know before you buy?

Because West Bay Club is a resale market, you will usually be working with a listing agent rather than a builder sales center. The questions that matter most are about the specific home: which associations it belongs to, what each one charges, whether any assessments are pending, and, for the towers, what the condominium association's reserves and inspections show. If you want the club, the membership terms are a separate set of questions.

What would we look at first if you called us about West Bay Club?

We would start by building the full monthly picture for the exact home, because it is never one number here. We would pull every association that applies, from the neighborhood to the master, and check for pending assessments. For a tower residence, we would look closely at the condominium documents, since those carry their own costs and timelines. If golf is important to you, we would find out how membership is being offered right now and what it would cost you after closing, so there are no surprises. We would also check the flood details for that address before you commit. That is the part we take care of for you, so you can focus on whether the home and the setting fit the way you want to live.

What this guide does not tell you

Club membership terms, fees and association costs change, and published sources do not always agree. This guide is general information, not legal or financial advice. Confirm current fees, membership terms and association details with the club, the associations and the documents for the specific home before you make an offer.

Frequently asked questions

Is golf membership required at West Bay Club?

No. Golf membership at West Bay Club is optional, so you can own a home there without joining the golf program. That is different from bundled communities such as Pelican Sound, where club membership comes with every home. Membership categories, initiation fees and dues change, so get the club's current schedule before you make an offer.

Where is West Bay Club?

West Bay Club is a gated community in Estero, about a mile west of US 41 on Williams Road. The roughly 868-acre property is bordered by the Estero River, Estero Bay and Halfway Creek, and about 500 acres of it are wetlands and nature preserve.

Does West Bay Club have beach access?

Yes. The community has a private beach club on Little Hickory Island, on Hickory Boulevard near Bonita Beach, which was rebuilt and reopened as a new facility. Ask the club or the association how access and any related fees work for the specific home you are considering.
