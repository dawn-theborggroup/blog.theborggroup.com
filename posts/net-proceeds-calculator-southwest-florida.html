Net proceeds calculator for selling your Southwest Florida home (2026): what you'll actually walk away with

By Mark & Dawn Borg·April 15, 2026·6 min read

What will you actually net after selling your home in Southwest Florida in 2026? Your net proceeds are the final amount you receive after commissions, taxes, closing costs, concessions, and your mortgage payoff are deducted — and in today's market, that number can be significantly different from your list price.

Why your net proceeds matter more than ever right now

Many sellers focus on list price. Smart sellers focus on what they walk away with. In today's Southwest Florida market — across Naples, Bonita Springs, Estero, Fort Myers, and Cape Coral — inventory has increased and buyer expectations have shifted. That means more negotiation and more pressure on pricing strategy. Understanding your net before you list gives you clarity, confidence, and control.

The simple net proceeds formula

Start with your expected sale price, then subtract: real estate commission, Florida documentary stamp tax, title insurance, closing and settlement fees, mortgage payoff, seller concessions, repairs or prep costs, and prorated property taxes. What remains is your true bottom line.

The costs most sellers underestimate

This is where surprises happen.

1. Documentary stamp tax

Florida charges $0.70 per $100 of the sale price. For example: a $500,000 sale = $3,500; a $750,000 sale = $5,250. This is a seller-paid cost.

2. Title insurance (seller-paid in most cases)

Typically ranges around $2,800-$3,200 on a $500K home.

3. Seller concessions (now common)

In today's market, 1-3% of sale price is typical — on a $500K home, that's a $5,000-$15,000 impact.

4. Mortgage payoff

Includes the remaining balance, interest, and possible fees. Always request an official payoff — don't estimate.

5. Pre-listing costs

These come out of pocket before you sell: paint, landscaping, repairs, cleaning and staging.

Example: what a seller might net

Here's a realistic snapshot: sale price $550,000; total costs combined (including mortgage payoff) approximately $333,400; estimated net approximately $216,600. This example shows the gap between price and profit is real — and why planning matters. A full breakdown is always available in your detailed net sheet, since every seller's mortgage balance and cost mix is different.

How your location impacts your net

Naples & Collier County

Higher prices mean higher taxes and title costs, and more complex HOA structures to account for.

Bonita Springs & Estero

HOA estoppel fees vary widely, and county differences affect closing structure.

Fort Myers & Cape Coral

More competition means more concessions, and new construction influences pricing strategy.

Why online calculators fall short

Most online tools use generic assumptions, ignore local negotiation trends, and miss HOA, insurance, and market variables. A true net sheet includes real-time local comps, current concession trends, and your actual mortgage payoff — that's the difference between a guess and a plan.
