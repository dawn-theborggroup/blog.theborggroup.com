Best Southwest Florida communities for boaters: Gulf access vs. lakes

By Mark & Dawn Borg·October 5, 2026·9 min read

What's the best Southwest Florida community for boaters? It depends on which kind of boating you want. For Gulf access, look at Bonita Bay in Bonita Springs and the Gulf-access neighborhoods of Naples. For gas-powered boating on freshwater, WildBlue in Estero and Vivid Shores in Bonita Springs stand out. For a quiet lake with electric boats and paddling, Corkscrew Shores is a strong fit. Start by deciding what kind of boater you are, then choose the community.

"Waterfront" means very different things here. In some communities it means a view from your lanai. In others it means backing a powerboat off your own dock. Here's how we sort it out for boaters.

Which communities have Gulf access?

If you want to boat to the Gulf of Mexico, you're looking at canal or bay communities rather than inland lakes. Bonita Bay has a full-service private marina with deep-water Gulf access, and select waterfront estates have private docks and lifts. It's a large, established community with five golf courses and more than 60 neighborhoods, and costs vary widely by neighborhood. See our Bonita Bay guide. In Naples and the north Bonita area, popular Gulf-access boating areas include Barefoot Beach, Vanderbilt Beach, and Park Shore, covered in our waterfront community guide.

At Pelican Landing, residents have a private barrier island beach park reached by a community boat shuttle, plus a kayak and canoe park. That's beach and paddle access rather than a dock at your door.

Before you buy any Gulf-access home, check bridge clearance and canal depth against your boat. They're easy to overlook and hard to fix after closing.

Which communities allow gas-powered boats on freshwater?

This is rare in Southwest Florida. Most inland gated communities restrict their lakes to electric motors. Two stand out:

  • WildBlue (Estero): navigable freshwater lakes where residents can powerboat, jet ski, and wakeboard, with private docks available on lakefront lots. Two of its three interconnected lakes allow jet skis and wakeboarding. Costs are real: HOA fees of roughly $500 to $667 a month plus a CDD. See our WildBlue guide.
  • Vivid Shores (Bonita Springs): more than 400 acres of boatable freshwater, with gas boats permitted and optional private docks on select homesites. HOA and CDD numbers hadn't been publicly disclosed when we last checked, so get them from the builder before you fall for a floor plan. See our Vivid Shores guide.

If a private dock matters, verify it before you go under contract. Dock feasibility depends on the lot's lake frontage, the phase, and the HOA's rules.

Where does electric-only boating make sense?

Not every boater needs a gas engine. Corkscrew Shores in Estero is built around a 240-acre former limerock lake with clear, deep water. You can kayak, canoe, paddleboard, fish catch-and-release, and run electric boats up to 16 feet. Gas-powered boating of any kind isn't allowed. It also has no CDD fee, which is a real cost advantage over WildBlue. Our Corkscrew Shores guide has the details.

For a different approach, San Carlos Estates in Bonita Springs is an AG-2 zoned acreage community with no HOA where boats, RVs, and horses are permitted on your property. It's a land-and-freedom play more than a marina lifestyle.

How should you choose?

  • Want the Gulf? Look at Bonita Bay and the Gulf-access neighborhoods of Naples. Budget for higher insurance and verify flood zones.
  • Want a gas boat or jet ski at home? Compare WildBlue and Vivid Shores, and get the fee structures in writing.
  • Want a quiet lake and lower fees? Corkscrew Shores is the standout.

Whatever you choose, ask about flood zone, insurance, dock rules, and lake or canal rules before you make an offer, and confirm current rules with the HOA. Waterfront insurance can swing your monthly cost more than the price of the home. We'll help you compare real numbers for your boat and budget. Common questions

What is the best Southwest Florida community for boaters?

It depends on the type of boating. Bonita Bay offers a private marina with Gulf access. WildBlue and Vivid Shores allow gas-powered boating on freshwater lakes. Corkscrew Shores offers a quiet lake limited to electric boats and paddling. Decide what you want to do on the water first, then compare communities.

Can you use a gas boat at Corkscrew Shores?

No. Corkscrew Shores' 240-acre lake allows kayaks, canoes, paddleboards, catch-and-release fishing, and electric-powered boats up to 16 feet, but no gas-powered boating of any kind. If you need a gas engine, look at WildBlue or Vivid Shores instead.

Do any Estero communities allow powerboats and jet skis?

Yes. WildBlue in Estero has navigable freshwater lakes where residents can powerboat, jet ski, and wakeboard, with private docks on eligible lakefront lots. Confirm dock eligibility and the HOA's rules before you make an offer.

Which community has Gulf of Mexico access for boats?

Bonita Bay in Bonita Springs has a full-service private marina with Gulf access, and select waterfront estates have private docks and lifts. Naples-area neighborhoods like Park Shore and Vanderbilt Beach are also popular for Gulf-access boating. Check bridge clearance and canal depth against your boat.

What should boaters check before buying a waterfront home in Southwest Florida?

Check the FEMA flood zone, the home's elevation certificate, roof age and hurricane protection, and homeowners and flood insurance quotes. For boats, also confirm bridge clearance, canal or lake depth, dock permitting, and the HOA's rules for your type of boat.
