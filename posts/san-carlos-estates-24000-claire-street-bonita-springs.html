What does $1,199,777 buy in San Carlos Estates, Bonita Springs?

At 24000 Claire Street, $1,199,777 buys a 4-bedroom, 3-bath, 2,482-square-foot custom pool home built in 2018, on 1.25 acres at the quiet end of Claire Street — with no HOA, no CDD, and AG-2 zoning. You get a heated pool under a 1,154-square-foot screened lanai, a 3-car garage, a 30-amp RV hookup with water, a metal roof, and whole-house PEX plumbing, all in FEMA Flood Zone X. It’s about 5 miles to downtown Bonita Springs, 4 to Coconut Point, and 8 to Bonita Beach.

That combination is hard to find anywhere in Southwest Florida, and almost impossible this close to the beach and town. Here’s what you’re buying, what makes San Carlos Estates different, and what to check before you write an offer.

What you get at 24000 Claire Street

The owners built this home in 2018 and chose the finishes themselves. It isn’t a spec home, and you can see it in the details.

The land and the freedom

The home sits at the very end of Claire Street, with no pass-through traffic, no home on one side, and a canal along one edge. The 1.25 acres are high, dry, and cleared, with new landscaping and full-property irrigation.

Because San Carlos Estates is zoned AG-2, the land works for how you want to live, not for an architectural review board. Boats, RVs, workshops, pole barns, a guest house, gardens, and animals are all allowed. There’s already a 30-amp RV hookup with water at the rear of the property.

The house

  • A kitchen built for gathering — granite counters, stainless appliances, rich cabinetry, and a breakfast bar that opens to the living and dining areas.
  • A primary suite set apart from the guest rooms, with a spa-inspired bath, walk-in shower, dual vanity, and a freestanding soaking tub.
  • A fourth bedroom that works as a guest suite, office, gym, or hobby room.
  • Marble-appointed bathrooms and diagonally laid Coretec luxury vinyl plank floors throughout.
  • A 3-car garage with epoxy floors and a Tonal smart gym.

The outdoor living

The heated pool, installed in 2020 and six feet deep, sits inside a 1,154-square-foot screened lanai with a brick-paver patio. It’s where you’ll spend your mornings with coffee and your evenings watching the sky change color over your own land.

Built for Florida

A metal roof, whole-house PEX plumbing, and R-30 blown attic insulation — the kind of construction details that matter to insurers, inspectors, and your utility bill. A 2018 build also means modern Florida code and newer systems, which makes the insurance file easier. Our post on four-point and wind mitigation inspections explains why that matters for your premium.

Why San Carlos Estates is different

Most of Southwest Florida is built around gated communities with HOA fees, CDD bonds, and rules about what you can park in your driveway. San Carlos Estates is the opposite.

  • $0 in HOA fees and no CDD. No monthly association bill and no bond assessment on your tax bill. Our guide on CDD fees in Bonita Springs and Estero shows how much that saves compared with many newer communities.
  • Acreage close to everything. Most lots run about 1.25 acres, inside Bonita Springs city limits. Other acreage communities in the region tend to be far out in rural Estero or unincorporated Lee County.
  • Flood Zone X. FEMA’s minimal-risk designation, so a federally backed lender won’t require flood insurance.
  • A neighborhood still growing. New custom estates in the $2 million to $4 million range have been going up throughout the community.

The streets are privately owned, with a semi-gated entry on one end that keeps through-traffic down. That’s worth a question before you buy, because private roads can carry a road assessment — we’ll check the tax bill for you. Our San Carlos Estates buyer’s guide covers the zoning, the roads, and what AG-2 allows in detail.

What it costs to own

With no HOA and no CDD, your carrying costs come down to property taxes, insurance, and upkeep on the land and pool. Your taxes reset based on your purchase price, so the sellers’ bill won’t be yours. If it’s your primary home, Florida’s homestead exemption and the Save Our Homes cap can lower the bill — our property tax calculator runs your number.

Who this home fits

It fits a buyer who wants room for an RV, a boat, a workshop, or a garden, without giving up a short drive to the beach, downtown, and Coconut Point. It fits anyone who’s tired of asking an HOA for permission. It fits less well if you want a gated community with a clubhouse and staff, or a lock-and-leave condo.

What would we look at first if you called us about this home?

We’d pull insurance quotes on the exact home — with a 2018 build, a metal roof, and Flood Zone X, that file should be a strong one — and we’d check the tax bill for any private road assessment. Then we’d talk through what you want to do with the land, from storing an RV to adding a workshop, so you know AG-2 covers it before you write an offer.
