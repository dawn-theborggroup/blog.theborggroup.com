Isles of Collier Preserve, Naples FL: what buyers need to know before making an offer in 2026

By Mark & Dawn Borg·September 2, 2026·9 min read

Isles of Collier Preserve is a 2,400-acre master-planned community in Naples, FL (34113) developed by Minto Communities, approximately 4 miles from downtown Naples near Rookery Bay Reserve. The builder is sold out — all purchases are resale only, meaning every transaction requires a buyer's agent. The community has no CDD fees, HOA costs of $170-$450/month depending on home type, and active listings ranging from $550K coastal cottages to $4.595M+ luxury single-family homes. With an average active days on market of 130 days and price reductions of $40K-$160K common in 2026, buyers have real negotiating room in this market.

Isles of Collier Preserve doesn't look like most Naples communities — and that's intentional. Where the typical Southwest Florida master-plan features Mediterranean rooflines, decorative columns, and manicured streetscapes meant to evoke Old World luxury, Isles of Collier is built around a coastal-inspired aesthetic: light-colored exteriors, metal roofs, covered lanais, and architecture that feels more Key West than Tuscany. The 2,400-acre development is woven through with 8 miles of kayak trails, biking paths, and hiking routes, with Rookery Bay National Estuarine Research Reserve at its back door.

If you're comparing this community to others in the Naples market, that context matters before we get to price per square foot.

Minto Communities delivered roughly 1,600 homes and then stopped taking new orders. Every home available in Isles of Collier Preserve today is a resale — which means you're not buying from a sales center with model upgrades and builder incentives. You're negotiating with individual sellers in a market that has cooled considerably since its 2022 peak. That shift matters for how you approach your search and your offer.

What you're buying (and what you'll pay)

The community offers three primary home categories.

Coastal Cottages and Villas are the entry point into the community, typically ranging from around $550,000 to $1.2M depending on size, upgrades, and location within the development. These are attached or detached smaller homes with HOA landscaping and irrigation included.

Coach Homes are two-story condominium-style buildings with attached garages, typically priced from the mid-$700Ks into the low $1M range. These share HOA expenses with neighboring units, which affects how the fee structure is calculated.

Single-Family Homes are the largest footprints in the community, ranging from approximately $1.5M on the lower end to $4.595M+ for waterfront or premium lots. These have the most variability in price, size, and condition, and the widest spread in days on market.

As of this writing, there are roughly 49 active listings across all three categories. The average listing price is around $1.4M, and the average price per square foot is approximately $606. Over the past six months, about 85 homes have sold at an average sale price near $1.5M. The gap between list price and average sale price, combined with an average active days-on-market of 130 days, tells you a lot about the current negotiating environment.

Isles of Collier Preserve has no CDD fees. That's not just a selling point in the brochure — it's a real cost differential that affects your monthly payment, your debt-to-income calculation, and your ability to qualify for certain loan products.

The no-CDD advantage, and what HOA actually covers

This is the number that separates Isles of Collier Preserve from most of its direct competitors. Community Development District (CDD) fees are an annual tax — collected as a line item on your property tax bill — used to repay the bonds that funded a community's infrastructure: roads, utilities, amenity construction. In a community like Fiddler's Creek or Verdana Village, CDD fees can add $3,000-$7,000+ per year to your carrying costs on top of HOA fees and property taxes. They don't disappear when the bond is paid — they often stay on the tax roll as ongoing operating district fees.

Isles of Collier Preserve has no CDD fees. HOA fees run between $170 and $450 per month depending on your home type and specific location in the community. The HOA is managed by FirstService Residential and covers landscaping, irrigation, and access to The Isles Club amenities.

The Isles Club is the community hub: a resort-style pool with a lap pool and private cabanas, tennis and pickleball courts, bocce ball, a fitness center, an event lawn, and the Overlook Bar & Grill — a waterfront restaurant inside a private community that's one of the more distinctive amenity offerings in Naples real estate. There's also a kayak and paddleboard launch directly onto the waterway system, with 8 miles of trails running through the preserve. This is the lifestyle piece that draws buyers here — the Overlook isn't a clubhouse bar, it's a real waterfront dining destination inside the gates.

What the market looks like right now

In 2026, Isles of Collier Preserve is solidly in buyer's market territory. With 49 active listings and a six-month absorption of about 85 homes, there's more than three months of available inventory at the current sales pace. Sellers who listed in 2023 or 2024 expecting to recapture peak pricing have been adjusting: price reductions of $40,000 to $160,000 are common depending on the price tier.

For buyers, this creates real opportunity — particularly in the single-family segment, where luxury listings have been sitting longest. The key is knowing which sellers are flexible and which ones are holding firm. That knowledge lives in the days-on-market data, the price reduction history, and the comparable sales, not in the listing itself. This is exactly the kind of analysis we run for every buyer before they make an offer on a community like this.

The buying process here: what's different in Florida

Because Isles of Collier Preserve is resale-only, you won't encounter the builder contract terms that buyers in new construction communities navigate — no deposit schedules, no build timelines, no upgrade pricing structures. You're working within standard Florida real estate contract terms.

In Florida, title companies handle closings, not attorneys. Your title company will conduct the title search, issue title insurance, and coordinate the closing. In Southwest Florida, sellers typically choose the title company, but it's negotiable.

Finally: if you're claiming the property as your primary residence, the homestead exemption removes $50,000 from the assessed value for property tax purposes and must be filed with Collier County by March 1 of the year following purchase. First-year owners often miss this deadline if no one flags it during closing.

How Isles of Collier compares

The two communities buyers most often cross-shop with Isles of Collier Preserve are Fiddler's Creek and Lely Resort — both in a similar price range and general Naples/Collier County location.

Fiddler's Creek has golf, country club dining, and a more resort-formal atmosphere. It also has CDD fees. Lely Resort's membership structure typically includes mandatory social or sports club dues layered on top of HOA fees.

Isles of Collier Preserve's value proposition is the inverse: coastal lifestyle, no mandatory club membership, no CDD. The amenities are excellent — but the carrying cost structure is simpler and more predictable. Whether that tradeoff works for you depends on your lifestyle priorities and your monthly payment math. Every situation is different, and the only way to know for sure is to run the numbers side-by-side on homes you're actually considering.
