What should you look for when choosing a buyer's agent in Naples or Fort Myers?

By Dawn Borg·May 28, 2026·5 min read

Naples and Fort Myers get talked about together constantly, but they're genuinely different markets — different price bands, different buyer profiles, different pace. The agent who's sharp on one isn't automatically sharp on the other. Here's what to actually look for.

Local knowledge means the sub-market, not the county

"I work Southwest Florida" isn't the same as knowing that a street in Olde Naples behaves nothing like a street ten minutes away, or that certain Fort Myers neighborhoods are in the middle of real revitalization while others have been stable for decades. Ask specific questions about the exact area you're considering and see how precise the answer is.

Speed matters more than most buyers expect

In a market that can move fast, the gap between "I'll get back to you" and a same-day showing can be the difference between seeing a home and finding out it's already under contract. Ask how quickly they can typically turn around a showing request, and pay attention to how quickly they respond to you during the interview itself — that's usually a preview of what working together looks like.

Who are they actually representing?

This is worth asking directly: are they a buyer's agent representing only you, or could they end up representing both sides of the same transaction? Florida allows transaction brokerage, where an agent can legally assist both buyer and seller without full representation of either — which isn't wrong, but you should know which relationship you're in before you're in it.

The best buyer's agent for you is the one who explains the buyer broker agreement clearly before asking you to sign it — not the one who rushes past it.

What to actually ask before you commit to an agent

  • How many transactions have you closed in this specific area in the past year?
  • Can you walk me through the buyer broker agreement before I sign it?
  • Who's your go-to inspector, lender, and title company, and why them?
  • How do you typically communicate — text, call, email — and how fast should I expect a response?
  • Will I be working with you directly, or handed off to someone else on your team?

The bottom line

The right agent for a Naples luxury purchase and the right agent for a Fort Myers first home aren't necessarily the same person. Look for someone who knows your specific target area cold, is upfront about who they represent, and explains the paperwork before asking for a signature — not after.
