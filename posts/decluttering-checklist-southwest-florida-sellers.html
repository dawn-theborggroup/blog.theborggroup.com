Seller Tips

Decluttering checklist for Southwest Florida sellers: maximize showings in a high-inventory market

By Mark & Dawn Borg·April 26, 2026·6 min read

What specific steps should Southwest Florida homeowners take to declutter before listing in today's competitive market? In a market with over 27,000 active listings, decluttered and photo-ready homes in Southwest Florida consistently generate more showings and stronger offers than homes that feel crowded or overly personalized.

Why decluttering matters more right now

Inventory across Bonita Springs, Estero, Naples, Fort Myers, and Cape Coral has expanded. Buyers have choices — and they're taking their time. When buyers walk into your home, they are comparing it to other resale homes, builder models that are professionally staged, and new construction communities with polished presentation.

You have one major advantage: a real home in a real neighborhood with real value. But buyers must be able to see that value instantly. Decluttering removes distractions, making your home feel larger, brighter, better maintained, and easier to move into. In today's market, that directly impacts showings and offers.

Room-by-room decluttering checklist

Living areas & great rooms

  • Remove at least 50% of décor and personal items
  • Open sightlines toward the lanai or pool
  • Eliminate cords, electronics clutter, and excess furniture
  • Reduce family photos to a few neutral pieces
  • Remove rugs if flooring is in good condition

Bathrooms

  • Remove all personal care items
  • Replace towels with clean, neutral sets
  • Recaulk grout if needed (critical in Florida humidity)
  • Clear cabinets and under-sink storage
  • Add one simple decorative item (plant or candle)

Garage & storage

  • Clear floors completely
  • Organize shelving and wall storage
  • Limit to one vehicle max during showings
  • Remove clutter to off-site storage if needed

Lanai, pool & outdoor living

  • Power wash pavers, deck, and screens
  • Remove pool toys and excess furniture
  • Keep pool water crystal clear
  • Trim landscaping inside the enclosure
  • Repair any damaged screens
This space sells the lifestyle — don't overlook it.

What to do with everything

Avoid moving clutter from room to room — buyers will find it. Use this system: 1. Donate or sell — items you won't take with you. 2. Pack & store — use climate-controlled storage, which matters in Southwest Florida's humidity. 3. Discard — anything broken, outdated, or unused.

Beyond decluttering: what buyers are watching

Today's buyers are also focused on hurricane protection — impact windows and shutters — and flooring condition. Decluttering gets buyers in the door. Condition and pricing get the home sold.

Where to focus first

If time is limited, focus first on the living room, kitchen, and primary bedroom — these drive the strongest first impressions. Staging is worth considering on top of decluttering, especially at higher price points where buyer expectations are elevated. Ideally, start 4-6 weeks before listing to give yourself time to sort, pack, and handle any small repairs the process turns up.

Final thoughts

Decluttering is one of the highest-return steps you can take before listing your home. It costs little, but directly impacts buyer perception, showing activity, and final sale price. In today's Southwest Florida market, preparation isn't optional — it's your advantage.
