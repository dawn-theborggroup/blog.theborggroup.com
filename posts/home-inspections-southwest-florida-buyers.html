What inspections matter most when buying a home in Southwest Florida?

By Mark & Dawn Borg·January 11, 2026·3 min read

Southwest Florida's climate makes inspections especially important. Buyers often ask which inspections truly matter.

Key inspections include:

  • General home inspection
  • Roof condition
  • Electrical and plumbing
  • HVAC systems
  • Wind mitigation
  • Flood history and elevation
  • Termite and pest inspection

Skipping or minimizing inspections can lead to costly surprises, particularly in older homes or coastal properties.

At The Borg Group, we help buyers interpret inspection results and negotiate repairs or credits strategically — protecting both the home and the investment.

A closer look at a few of these

Wind mitigation deserves special attention, since it directly affects your insurance premium — see our guide to 4-point and wind mitigation inspections for what inspectors check and how the discounts work. Flood history and elevation matter just as much for your ongoing insurance costs; our guide to flood insurance costs in Southwest Florida breaks down what drives that number.
