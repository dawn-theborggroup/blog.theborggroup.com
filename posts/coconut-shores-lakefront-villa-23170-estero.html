Coconut Shores lakefront villa in Estero: what does $445,800 buy? 2026

By Mark & Dawn Borg·November 18, 2026·8 min read

What does $445,800 buy in Coconut Shores, Estero?

At 23170 Coconut Shores Drive, $445,800 buys a 3-bedroom, 2-bath lakefront villa of about 1,704 square feet at the quiet end of the lake, with a long water view down to the community fountain and a preserve along one side. The roof was replaced in 2020, the primary bath has been fully remodeled, and the home sits in FEMA Flood Zone X. The villa association fee is $1,353 a quarter, and you’re in a gated community just west of US-41 on Coconut Road, a few minutes from Coconut Point.

That’s the short answer. The longer one is about why this villa is priced where it is, because Coconut Shores villas are similar on paper — most are three bedrooms and two baths at roughly 1,700 square feet, built from 2000 to 2002. What separates them is the view, the roof, and what’s been updated inside. Here’s how this one stacks up, and what to check before you write an offer.

What you’re paying for at 23170 Coconut Shores Drive

We tell every buyer looking in Coconut Shores the same thing: in a community this uniform, the setting is the price. Here’s what this villa brings.

The view people stop and look at

Most lake lots in Coconut Shores look across the water at the villas on the other side. This one looks down the long end of the lake, so the view runs the length of the water to the fountain in the distance, with a lush privacy island in front. A preserve on one side adds a buffer you won’t get on an interior lot.

It’s a pie-shaped lot, which is why it feels more open than the villa footprint suggests. You share one wall with your neighbor, like every villa here, and the other side faces trees.

The updates that matter in Florida

  • Roof replaced in 2020. With these villas now 24 to 26 years old, roof age moves both price and insurability. A 2020 roof is one of the first things an insurer and an inspector will ask about.
  • Remodeled primary bath. An oversized walk-in shower, upgraded counters, and an accessibility-friendly layout.
  • Electric hurricane shutter on the lanai. It closes off the lanai from the home at the push of a button — useful for seasonal owners who lock up and head north.
  • Brick-paver walkway to the backyard.

Inside, there’s tile in the main living areas, luxury vinyl in the primary bedroom, and carpet in the two guest rooms. The kitchen has stainless appliances and opens to the living and dining areas, and the screened lanai is where you’ll spend your mornings.

How the price compares

Coconut Shores villa sales this year have closed from about $385,000 to $425,000, and most of those homes had wooded or garden views. At $445,800, this villa sits above that range, and that’s the point: you’re paying for the long lake view, the preserve buffer, the 2020 roof, and the remodeled bath together. Whether that premium is right for you depends on how much those four things are worth to you — and that’s a conversation we’re happy to have with the comps in front of you.

If you want the bigger picture on the community, our Coconut Shores buyer’s guide covers villas versus condos, fees, and the full sales history.

Flood zone, fees, and the true cost of owning

Flood Zone X. The home is in FEMA’s Zone X, the area of minimal flood hazard, so a federally backed lender won’t require flood insurance. You may see “Evacuation Zone A” on the property record — that’s Lee County’s hurricane evacuation map, not a flood zone, and it doesn’t set your insurance requirement. Our guide on how to check flood risk in Estero explains the difference.

One thing to plan for: if you insure the villa through Citizens, a flood policy is required from January 1, 2027, regardless of zone. Our post on the Citizens flood requirement covers the details.

Association fee. $1,353 a quarter, about $5,412 a year, for access to the heated pool and spa, clubhouse, fitness center, tennis and pickleball courts, the lakeside tiki lounge, and the fishing pier. There’s no golf membership in the monthly math.

Property taxes. Your taxes reset based on your purchase price, so the seller’s current bill won’t be yours. If it’s your primary home, Florida’s homestead exemption and the Save Our Homes cap can make a real difference — our property tax calculator runs your number.

What’s happening on Coconut Road

The street itself is changing. At the US-41 corner across from Coconut Point, Woodfield Estero has broken ground on a 45.6-acre mixed-use project with apartments, a boutique hotel, shops, and a public park, with the first residences expected in the third quarter of 2027. Toward the water, the Ritz-Carlton Residences, Estero Bay are rising within the 500-acre Saltleaf community, with a marina and the new Saltleaf Golf Preserve. Coconut Shores sits between the two, behind its own gate.

Day to day, Coconut Point’s 125-plus shops and restaurants are minutes away, Barefoot Beach and Bonita Beach Park are about 7.5 miles off, and Southwest Florida International Airport is roughly 20 to 25 minutes north.

Who this villa fits

It fits a buyer who wants a single-level, lock-and-leave home with a real amenity package, a water view you won’t get tired of, and a newer roof. It fits less well if you want a detached home, a private pool, or room to park a boat. Pet and rental rules come from the villa association, so we’ll get you the current documents before you commit.

What would we look at first if you called us about this home?

We’d pull insurance quotes on the exact villa — with the 2020 roof, that file should be a strong one — and we’d line up the recent villa sales so you can see the view premium for yourself. Then we’d walk you through the association documents and the approval timeline, so there are no surprises between your offer and your closing.
