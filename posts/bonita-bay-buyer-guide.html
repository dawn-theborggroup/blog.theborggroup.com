Bonita Bay: what buyers need to know about Southwest Florida's premier waterfront community

By Mark & Dawn Borg·September 15, 2026·6 min read

Bonita Bay is a 2,400-acre master-planned gated community with 90 holes of championship golf across five courses, a full-service marina with Gulf of Mexico access, a private beach club, 10 pickleball courts, an 18-court tennis facility, a fitness center and spa, and 12 miles of biking and walking paths. The community has over 60 distinct neighborhoods within it — condos, coach homes, single-family homes, and waterfront estates. Prices range from roughly $600,000 for condos to $8–9 million or more for top waterfront properties.

If comprehensive amenities in a gated setting are your priority, Bonita Bay is the benchmark against which most Southwest Florida communities get compared. It's not a new build — it's an established, mature community with decades of landscaping, infrastructure, and social culture behind it.

What sets Bonita Bay apart

The defining feature is scale combined with genuine waterfront access. Five championship golf courses is more than most communities in the region offer combined, and the marina — with deep-water Gulf access — means residents with boats aren't limited to a canal or an inland lake. Select waterfront estates include private docks and lifts directly on the property.

Beyond golf and boating, the amenity base runs deep: a private beach club, 10 pickleball courts, an 18-court tennis facility, a full fitness center and spa, and 12 miles of trails for biking and walking. The Promenade at Bonita Bay adds a public-facing waterfront dining and shopping district within the community itself.

Over 60 distinct neighborhoods sit within Bonita Bay's boundaries — condos, coach homes, single-family homes, and waterfront estates — which means the right fit within the community depends heavily on which neighborhood you're looking at, not just the community name.

What to know before you look

Because Bonita Bay is both large and long-established, condition and location within the community vary significantly from one neighborhood to the next — an older, unrenovated condo and a newly built waterfront estate can carry the "Bonita Bay" name while representing entirely different price points and buyer profiles. Golf and club membership structures also vary by property and should be confirmed specifically for any home you're considering, since membership rules differ meaningfully from one Southwest Florida golf community to the next.

As with any established gated community in Bonita Springs, expect an HOA buyer approval process — application, background check, and in some cases a board interview. See our HOA approval guide for what to expect and how to budget the extra time into your closing timeline.
